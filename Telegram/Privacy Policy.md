# FurrCreators Telegram Bot Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** July 10, 2026

## Overview

FurrCreators ("the Bot") is a Telegram bot owned and operated by Furractions.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, how it is stored, and your rights regarding your data when interacting with the Bot.

By using the Bot, you agree to the terms described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information required for the Bot to function properly. This may include:

* Your Telegram User ID (required for identifying your account within the Bot system)
* Your Telegram username (if provided by Telegram)
* Account linking or verification status
* Basic operational metadata required for Bot functionality
* Commands sent to the Bot for processing

We do **not** collect:

* Passwords
* Financial information
* Payment details
* Banking information
* Device information
* Contacts or address books
* Personal files or photos
* Private Telegram conversations unrelated to the Bot

---

## Message Processing and Logging

When you send commands or messages to the Bot, those messages may temporarily appear in the terminal or console running the Bot software on the server hosting the Bot.

These messages are only used for:

* Processing commands
* Providing Bot functionality
* Debugging issues
* Diagnosing errors
* Preventing abuse, spam, or malicious activity
* Maintaining service reliability

Your messages:

* **Will never be shared with third parties**
* **Will never be sold to advertisers, advertising agencies, or marketing companies**
* **Will never be used for AI model training**
* **Will never be submitted for human review for training purposes**
* **Will never be intentionally published or distributed**

Access to these logs is limited to the Bot operator and only when necessary for maintenance or troubleshooting purposes.

---

## How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for:

* Providing core Bot functionality
* Identifying users within the Bot system
* Processing commands and generating responses
* Account linking and verification systems
* Maintaining reliability and uptime
* Preventing abuse, spam, fraud, or malicious activity

We do **not** use your information for:

* Advertising
* Marketing campaigns
* User profiling
* Behavioral tracking
* Selling data
* Cross-platform tracking

---

## We Never Sell Your Data

Furractions does **not** sell, rent, trade, license, or otherwise provide your information to:

* Advertising companies
* Advertising agencies
* Marketing firms
* Data brokers
* Analytics resellers
* Third-party advertisers

**We never sell your data to advertising agents or advertising platforms.**

Your information is used solely for operating and maintaining the FurrCreators Telegram Bot and its associated services.

---

## Data Storage and Infrastructure

The FurrCreators Telegram Bot is self-hosted and operated directly by Furractions.

User information is stored exclusively in a SQLite database powered by Turso.

Turso acts solely as a database infrastructure provider and does not own your data.

No third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services are used by the Bot.

---

## Data Retention

We retain data only for as long as necessary for the Bot to function properly and provide its services.

Data may be automatically removed when:

* It is no longer operationally required.
* The associated account becomes inactive.
* A valid deletion request is processed.

Certain minimal records may be retained temporarily for:

* Abuse prevention
* Security investigations
* Fraud prevention
* Legal compliance requirements

Any retained information is removed once it is no longer required.

* Secure storage practices
* Protection against unauthorized modification or disclosure

By using the Bot, you acknowledge and accept this limitation.
