# Furractions Documents

Official legal, policy, compliance, and governance documents for Furractions services and projects.

This repository serves as the central location for all public-facing documents used across the Furractions ecosystem.

---

## 📖 About Furractions

Furractions develops tools, platforms, and services for the furry community, creators, artists, commissioners, fursuit makers, and community members.

This repository exists to provide transparency regarding:

- User rights and responsibilities
- Privacy practices
- Service terms
- Community standards
- Verification procedures
- Platform policies
- Operational guidelines

---

## 📄 Available Documents

### Privacy Policy

The Privacy Policy explains:

- What information we collect
- How information is used
- How information is stored
- Security measures we implement
- User privacy rights
- Third-party services we utilize

### Terms of Service

The Terms of Service defines:

- Rules for using Furractions services
- User responsibilities
- Acceptable and prohibited conduct
- Service limitations
- Account management policies
- Enforcement actions

### Acceptable Use Policy

The Acceptable Use Policy outlines prohibited activities including abuse, fraud, spam, and attempts to compromise platform security.

### Community Guidelines

Community Guidelines describe expected behavior and standards for participating in Furractions communities and services.

### Verification Policy

The Verification Policy explains creator verification requirements, review procedures, eligibility requirements, and verification limitations.

### Account Recovery Policy

The Account Recovery Policy explains how users may request assistance recovering access to their Furractions accounts.
